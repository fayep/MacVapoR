MacVapoR bundles software from several projects, each under its own licence.

alvrxr (the OpenXR runtime, libalvrxr.dylib) and the MacVapoR app
  https://github.com/fayep/MacVapoR
  MIT licence, see MacVapoR-MIT.txt.

ALVR server_core (libalvr_server_core.dylib), ALVR 20.14.1
  https://github.com/alvr-org/ALVR
  MIT licence, see ALVR-MIT.txt. It is built from Rust crates under their own permissive
  licences (MIT, Apache-2.0 and similar), listed in ALVR's Cargo.lock.

OpenXR loader (libopenxr_loader.dylib), from the Khronos OpenXR-SDK-Source
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenXR-SDK-Source, with the patch in
  https://github.com/fayep/OpenXR-SDK-Source/tree/macos-alvrxr
  Apache-2.0, see OpenXR-loader-Apache-2.0.txt.

wineopenxr (wineopenxr.dll, wineopenxr.so), from Valve's Proton at commit
  164e0ccd2ea2b1ec2e5d08dc97f65b184c2539ca, built for CrossOver with
  wineopenxr-crossover.patch, from https://github.com/fayep/NotProton/tree/wineopenxr
  BSD-3-Clause (Proton's LICENSE.proton); its generated thunk files are Apache-2.0 OR MIT.

OpenComposite is not included. MacVapoR downloads it on request from its official builds,
https://gitlab.com/znixian/OpenOVR (GPL-3.0).
